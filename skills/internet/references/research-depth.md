# Research Depth

Use depth as a contract with the user. More depth means more iterations, broader source coverage, stronger contradiction handling, and a more explicit evidence trail.

## Quick

Use for simple fact checks, narrow current lookups, and tasks where the user clearly wants speed.

- Search passes: 1-2.
- Sources opened: 3-5.
- Required mix: at least one authoritative or primary source when available, plus one independent corroborating source for material claims.
- Contradiction work: one targeted query when the claim affects a decision.
- Output: direct answer with inline citations and one caveat sentence if needed.
- Stop when the answer is verified by the minimum source mix.

## Standard

Use by default for web research.

- Search passes: 3-4.
- Sources opened: 8-15.
- Required mix: primary source, independent source, community or ground-truth source when relevant, and contradiction search.
- Claim ledger: track material claims, dates, confidence, and caveats.
- Output: bottom line, findings, uncertainty, and recommendation.
- Stop when core claims have support and remaining gaps are minor or inaccessible.

## Deep

Use when the user asks for detailed, exhaustive, serious, report-grade, due diligence, "anything required", investigation, comparison, market map, or when a decision is high-cost or high-stakes.

- Search passes: 5-8.
- Sources opened: 20+ when available.
- Required mix: primary records, independent analysis, community/lived-experience evidence, contradiction sources, historical/archive sources when timeline matters, and source-specific searches.
- Claim ledger: required.
- Source inventory: required.
- Contradiction ledger: required.
- Output: executive summary, method, findings, evidence table or claim ledger, conflicts, gaps, and next actions.
- Stop only after a saturation test and final gap pass.

## Exhaustive

Use only when explicitly requested or when the expected output is an audit-grade artifact.

- Search passes: continue until saturation by source class.
- Sources opened: as many as needed, grouped by source type and inclusion criteria.
- Required extras: query ledger, source inclusion/exclusion notes, evidence table, contradiction table, inaccessible-source list, and reproducible methodology.
- Output: full report or evidence pack.
- Stop when three consecutive targeted searches produce no new material evidence and every planned source class is either covered or explicitly unavailable.

## Escalation Rules

Move from quick to standard when:

- The user asks "latest", "best", "compare", "should I", "is this true", "what changed", or "verify".
- Sources disagree.
- The answer could affect spending, travel, business, health, legal, financial, security, or safety decisions.

Move from standard to deep when:

- The prompt is broad, ambiguous, competitive, investigative, or high-impact.
- The answer depends on multiple entities or jurisdictions.
- The source trail crosses web, docs, social, code, filings, videos, PDFs, and official records.
- The user asks for report-grade output or "as detailed as possible".

## Saturation Test

Before synthesis, confirm:

- Three consecutive targeted searches produced no materially new source, claim, entity, date, or contradiction.
- The strongest counterclaim has been searched.
- The newest update has been searched.
- Official source plus exact claim terms has been searched.
- Date-bounded searches were used when recency matters.
- Remaining gaps are named and classified as unknown, inaccessible, paywalled, private, or out of scope.
